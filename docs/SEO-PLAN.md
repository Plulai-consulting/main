# SEO plan: PlulAI Consulting across the GCC

No one can promise a #1 ranking, and rankings take months. This plan builds everything that is under your control and tells you what to do next.

## Already built into the site
- Fast, static pages (small Inter font file, no heavy scripts). Good for Google's speed checks.
- 20 pages: Home, Marketing, AI solutions, Social tool, plus Qatar, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman, each in English and Arabic.
- One address per page, a canonical link, and English/Arabic `hreflang` links so Google shows the right language in the right country.
- Titles and descriptions written per page, one H1 per page, and a share image for WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X.
- Structured data (Organization, WebSite, Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb) so search engines and AI answers understand the business.
- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` (keeps login, portal and admin out of search) and `llms.txt` (for AI search tools).

## Do these first (in order)
1. Set your real domain: add `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://your-domain` to your hosting settings and redeploy.
2. Add the site to **Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster Tools**. Submit `/sitemap.xml`. Check "Pages" weekly for errors.
3. Add your social profile links to the Organization data (`sameAs`) in the script once those profiles exist.
4. Create a **Google Business Profile** only for places where you have a real, visitable address. Never invent one.
5. Keep your name, phone and email identical everywhere (site, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, directories).

## Keywords to test (hypotheses, not verified volumes)
Check each in Google Keyword Planner or Search Console before investing.
- English: marketing agency Qatar, AI consulting Qatar, social media marketing Doha, AI consultant Dubai, digital marketing agency Riyadh, AI automation for small business GCC.
- Arabic: وكالة تسويق في قطر، استشارات الذكاء الاصطناعي، تسويق رقمي الرياض، إدارة حسابات السوشال ميديا الدوحة، أتمتة الأعمال بالذكاء الاصطناعي.

Arabic searches often face less competition, which makes them your best early opening.

## What actually moves rankings (the long game)
1. **Proof pages.** Add 3 to 5 real case studies (business type, problem, what you did, a result you can honestly state). This is the biggest gap and the biggest lever.
2. **Helpful articles**, 2 per month, in both languages, answering what owners search ("how often should a restaurant post in Ramadan", "what can AI automate for a clinic"). Link each to the matching country and service page.
3. **Links from trusted sites:** local business directories, chambers of commerce, partner websites, podcasts or interviews, and your clients' sites. A few good links beat many weak ones.
4. **Reviews and testimonials** (with permission), shown on the site and on Google if you have a profile.
5. **Keep country pages honest.** Keep a country page only if you truly serve that market, and keep adding genuine local detail and examples. Near-copies across countries can hurt you.

## Measure
Search Console (queries, pages, countries), Bing Webmaster, and a simple monthly note: impressions, clicks, top 10 keywords, new links, and enquiries that came from search.

## 90-day rhythm
- Weeks 1 to 2: domain, Search Console, Bing, profiles, first 2 case studies.
- Weeks 3 to 8: 4 articles (2 English, 2 Arabic), 5 directory or partner links, collect reviews.
- Weeks 9 to 12: review Search Console, improve pages that get impressions but few clicks (better titles), double down on what works.
